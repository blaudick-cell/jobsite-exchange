Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jobsite Exchange, LLC (“JobsiteExchange,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use jobsiteexchange.com and our related services (the “Service”) to post and browse listings for surplus and needed earth materials such as clean fill, topsoil, aggregate, and concrete.

1.Information we collect

  • Listing information. Details you submit when posting, including jobsite location, material type, quantity, availability or timeline, and whether you have or need material.
  • Contact information. Email address and/or phone number used to publish a listing, manage it, and connect with other jobsites.
  • Listing-management credentials. One-time codes (OTPs) and manage codes we send by email so you can edit or remove your own listings.
  • Usage and device data. Standard log data such as IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and approximate location, collected automatically when you use the Service.

2.How we use information

  • Operate the marketplace and publish, match, and display listings.
  • Connect parties who have material with parties who need it.
  • Send transactional messages such as OTPs, manage codes, and payment confirmations.
  • Process payments for featured or promoted listings.
  • Maintain security, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Improve and analyze the Service.

3.How we share information

  • With other users. Listing details you choose to publish (including location and the contact method you provide) are visible to other jobsites so they can reach you about a haul.
  • Service providers. Vendors that host the site, deliver email, display maps, and process payments — for example [Netlify, your email provider, your map provider].
  • Legal and safety. When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and property of JobsiteExchange, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information.

4.Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the Service working, to remember preferences, and to understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work without them.

5.Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for legitimate business or legal purposes. You may request deletion of your listings and associated contact information at any time (see Contact below).

6.Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at Dylan@jobsiteexchange.com. We will respond as required by applicable law.

7.Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8.Children’s privacy

The Service is intended for businesses and individuals aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and revise the “Last updated” date above.

10.Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at Dylan@jobsiteexchange.com or 2535 River Drive, Denver, Colorado 80211.
